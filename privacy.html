Privacy Policy - Gizlilik Politikası

Mario TR Games provides this application as a free mobile game. This Service is provided by Mario TR Games and is intended for use as is.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, how it is used, and which third-party services are used when you use the application.

Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, the application may process or store the following information:

  • Google Play Games player display name
  • Google Play Games authentication information
  • Firebase user identifier (UID)
  • Selected player icon or avatar identifier
  • Game progress, including level and XP
  • Earned stars and other gameplay statistics
  • Leaderboard information
  • Advertising identifiers where permitted
  • Device and operating system information
  • Diagnostic and crash information

Google Play Games

The application may use Google Play Games Services to allow players to sign in using their Google Play Games profile.

When you sign in, your Google Play Games display name may be used inside the application and may be displayed on the game's leaderboard.

The application does not require access to your Google account password. Authentication is handled by Google Play Games Services.

Firebase Authentication

The application uses Firebase Authentication to associate a Google Play Games account with an application user account.

Firebase may generate a unique user identifier (UID) that is used to identify your game profile and synchronize your leaderboard and progress information.

Firebase Realtime Database and Leaderboards

The application may store game-related information in Firebase Realtime Database. This information is used to provide online features such as leaderboards and player profiles.

Stored information may include:

  • Firebase UID
  • Player display name
  • Selected player icon
  • XP
  • Current or highest unlocked level
  • Star balance or gameplay statistics
  • Last synchronization time

Leaderboard information such as your player display name, selected icon, level and XP may be visible to other players using the leaderboard feature.

Local Game Data

Some game preferences and progress information may also be stored locally on your device. This may include settings such as sound, vibration, selected icon and offline game progress.

Advertising

The application may use Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob and its advertising partners may process information such as advertising identifiers, device information, IP address and interaction data in accordance with applicable privacy settings and user consent.

Third-Party Services

The application uses third-party services that may collect and process information according to their own privacy policies.

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Play Games Services
  • Firebase Privacy and Security
  • Google AdMob

Log Data

In the event of an application error, third-party services may collect diagnostic information. This information may include device information, operating system version, application configuration, IP address, date and time of use and technical error information.

Data Sharing

Mario TR Games does not sell your personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers such as Google, Firebase and AdMob when required to provide authentication, leaderboard, advertising, analytics or technical services.

Data Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect information processed by the application. Communication with supported online services is transmitted using encrypted connections such as HTTPS.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Data Retention and Deletion

Game profile and leaderboard information may remain stored while the associated online profile is active or while it is required to provide the leaderboard service.

If you would like to request deletion of online game profile information associated with your account, you may contact us using the email address listed below.

Local game data can also be removed by clearing the application's data or by using available reset options inside the application.

Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed to children under the age of 13. Mario TR Games does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If we become aware that personal information from a child under 13 has been collected, reasonable steps will be taken to delete it.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when application features, third-party services or legal requirements change. Users are encouraged to review this page periodically.
