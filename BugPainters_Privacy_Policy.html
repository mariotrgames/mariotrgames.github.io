Privacy Policy - Bug Painters

Bug Painters is developed by Mario TR Games. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when you use the game, why it is used, and which third-party services may be involved.

We aim to use only the information needed to provide the game, display advertisements, maintain security, and support the app's technical operation.

1. Gameplay Data

Bug Painters may store gameplay information on your device so the game can remember your progress and settings. This may include:

  • Completed levels and game progress
  • In-game score, currency, and upgrades
  • Game settings and preferences

This gameplay data is stored locally on your device. It may be deleted if you clear the app's storage or uninstall the game. The current version of the game does not require you to create an account to play.

2. Advertising and Device Information

Bug Painters uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Depending on your device, region, consent choices, and Google settings, Google and its advertising partners may process information such as:

  • Advertising identifiers and device identifiers
  • Device model, operating system, and app version
  • IP address, which may be used to estimate general location
  • Interactions with the app and advertisements
  • Diagnostic, performance, and fraud-prevention information

This information may be used to deliver and measure advertisements, limit invalid activity, prevent fraud, and maintain advertising services. Whether ads are personalized depends on applicable law, your consent choices, and your device or Google account settings.

Your choices: You can manage advertising and privacy preferences through your Android device settings and, where available or required, through privacy choices in the game. You may also review your Google ad settings.

3. Third-Party Services

Bug Painters uses Google AdMob for advertising. Google may process data according to its own privacy policies, service terms, and your account or device settings.

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services
  • Google AdMob privacy information

4. Log and Diagnostic Data

Google, Android, or other platform services may process technical and diagnostic information needed to deliver ads, maintain security, or identify service errors. Depending on the service, this may include device and app information, error details, and network information.

5. Data Sharing and Use

Mario TR Games does not sell your gameplay progress or support messages. Information may be processed by Google AdMob and platform providers where needed for:

  • Displaying and measuring advertisements
  • Fraud prevention and service security
  • Operating and troubleshooting the game
  • Responding to support requests you send us

Advertising providers may handle data under their own policies. The meaning of “sale” or “sharing” can differ under privacy laws.

6. Data Retention and Security

Gameplay data stored locally remains on your device until you delete the app's data or uninstall the game. Support emails are retained only for as long as reasonably needed to respond to and follow up on your request. Google determines retention for data it processes through AdMob under its own policies.

Reasonable safeguards are used to protect information under our control. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. International Data Processing

Google and its service providers may process information in countries other than the one where you live. Such processing is governed by Google's privacy terms and applicable data protection laws.

8. Children's Privacy

Bug Painters does not ask players to provide their name, email address, or other direct personal information to play. The game's target age group and advertising settings should match the audience selected in the app store. If the game is directed to children, applicable child privacy and family advertising requirements must be followed.

If you believe that a child has sent us personal information, please contact us so we can review the request and take appropriate action.

Depending on where you live and which laws apply, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing. You may also have the right to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

To make a privacy request, contact us using the email address below and include “Privacy Request - Bug Painters” in the subject line. We may need to verify your identity before responding. You can also use the privacy and advertising controls offered by Google or your device.
