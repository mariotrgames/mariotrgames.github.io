Privacy Policy - OmniWord

OmniWord was built by Mario TR Games as a free application. This Service is provided at no cost and is intended for use as is.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when you use OmniWord, why it is used, and which third-party services are involved.

OmniWord is designed to collect only the information necessary to provide game functionality, leaderboards, advertising, security, and technical operation of the application.

1. Google Play Games Sign-In

OmniWord may offer optional sign-in through Google Play Games Services.

Google Play Games sign-in is used only to identify the player for game-related features such as the OmniWord leaderboard. Signing in is not required to play the main game.

Important: OmniWord does not request, read, or store your Google account password, Gmail messages, contacts, Google Drive files, photos, payment information, or other private Google account content.

When you sign in with Google Play Games, OmniWord may use the following game-related information:

  • Google Play Games player/display name
  • A unique Firebase user identifier (UID)
  • Your OmniWord star score
  • The selected language combination used for leaderboard ranking

Your Google Play Games player name may be displayed to other OmniWord players in the leaderboard together with your star score.

Google Play Games itself may process additional information according to Google's own privacy policies and your Google Play Games privacy settings.

2. Leaderboard Data

OmniWord provides competitive leaderboards for different language combinations.

For this purpose, the following information may be stored in Firebase Realtime Database:

  • Firebase user identifier (UID)
  • Google Play Games player name
  • Current star score
  • Selected language pair
  • Last score update time

This information is used only to calculate and display leaderboard rankings and to prevent one player from modifying another player's leaderboard entry.

OmniWord does not use leaderboard information for advertising targeting or sell leaderboard information to third parties.

3. Firebase Authentication

OmniWord uses Firebase Authentication to securely link a Google Play Games player with a unique Firebase user account.

Authentication is handled by Google and Firebase. OmniWord does not receive or store your Google account password.

The Firebase UID is used internally to distinguish one player from another and to protect leaderboard records.

4. Advertising and Device Information

OmniWord uses Google AdMob to display banner, interstitial, and rewarded advertisements.

Depending on your device, region, consent choices, and Google services, advertising services may process information such as:

  • Advertising identifiers
  • Device information
  • Operating system information
  • Approximate location derived from network information
  • Advertising interactions
  • Diagnostic and performance information

Rewarded advertisements are optional. Players may choose to watch a rewarded advertisement in exchange for an in-game star reward.

5. Third-Party Services

OmniWord uses services provided by Google. These services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Play Games Privacy and Profile Settings
  • Firebase Privacy and Security
  • Google AdMob Privacy Information

6. Log and Diagnostic Data

If the application encounters an error, third-party SDKs and platform services may collect diagnostic information necessary to detect, troubleshoot, and improve technical problems.

Such information may include:

  • Device type and operating system
  • Application version
  • Error and diagnostic information
  • Time and date of application activity
  • Network and technical information

7. Data Security

Information transmitted between OmniWord and Google/Firebase services is transmitted using secure network connections.

Firebase security rules are used to restrict leaderboard modifications so that authenticated players may modify only their own leaderboard records.

No method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure, but reasonable technical safeguards are used to protect information.

8. Children’s Privacy

OmniWord does not intentionally request children to provide names, email addresses, passwords, contact information, or other direct personal information.

Google Play Games, Firebase, Google Play Services, and AdMob may process information according to their own policies, account settings, age requirements, and applicable laws.

If we become aware that personal information has been collected in a manner that should not have occurred, appropriate steps will be taken to remove it where applicable.

9. Data Use and Sharing

OmniWord does not sell players' personal information.

Game-related information is processed only where necessary for:

  • Google Play Games authentication
  • Leaderboard functionality
  • Saving and displaying player scores
  • Advertising functionality
  • Security and fraud prevention
  • Technical operation and troubleshooting

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when OmniWord features or third-party services change.

Users are encouraged to review this page periodically for updates. Changes become effective when the updated Privacy Policy is published on this page.
