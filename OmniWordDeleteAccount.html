OmniWord Account & Data Deletion

This page explains how users of OmniWord, developed by Mario TR Games, can request deletion of their OmniWord account data or game data.

Delete OmniWord Account and Associated Data

If you want your OmniWord account data deleted, send a request to:

Email: mustfgncc@gmail.com

Please include the following information:

  • Your Google Play Games player name
  • State that you want your OmniWord account and associated game data deleted

The following OmniWord data may be deleted:

  • Firebase user identifier associated with OmniWord
  • Google Play Games display name stored in the OmniWord leaderboard
  • Leaderboard star scores
  • Language-pair leaderboard records

Deleting OmniWord data does not delete your Google account or Google Play Games account.

Delete Game Data Without Deleting the Account

You may also request deletion of leaderboard and game-related data without requesting deletion of the associated authentication account.

Send an email to: mustfgncc@gmail.com

Please state that you want only your OmniWord game data deleted.

Retention

Deletion requests will be processed within a reasonable period after the request is verified. Certain technical or security records may be retained temporarily where required for fraud prevention, legal compliance, or system integrity.
