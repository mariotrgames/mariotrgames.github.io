Privacy Policy — Bug Defense

Last updated: August 7, 2026

MarioTrGame built Bug Defense as an ad-supported mobile game. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and shared when you use the game.

By using Bug Defense, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

Information We Collect

Bug Defense does not require users to create an account or provide a name, email address, phone number, or other direct personal information in order to play.

However, third-party services used by the game may automatically collect certain technical and device-related information, including:

  • Device information
  • Advertising identifiers
  • IP address
  • Approximate location derived from IP address
  • Operating system and device model
  • App version
  • Advertising interaction data
  • Diagnostic and technical information
  • Information related to consent choices

The exact information collected may depend on the user's device, region, privacy choices, and the third-party services used by the application.

Advertising

Bug Defense uses Google AdMob to display advertisements.

  • Banner advertisements
  • Interstitial advertisements
  • Rewarded advertisements

Rewarded advertisements are optional advertisements that may provide an in-game reward after the advertisement is successfully completed.

Google and its advertising partners may process device and advertising information to provide, measure, limit the frequency of, and report advertisements. Depending on your region and privacy choices, advertisements may be personalized or non-personalized.

Consent and European Privacy Regulations

Bug Defense uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage advertising consent where required.

Users located in regions such as the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, and Switzerland may be presented with a privacy and consent message before certain advertising technologies are used.

The consent message may allow users to choose whether their information can be used for purposes such as personalized advertising.

Non-Personalized Advertisements

If personalized advertising consent is not available or has not been granted, Google may provide non-personalized or otherwise privacy-restricted advertisements where supported.

Non-personalized advertising does not use past user behavior for ad personalization, although certain device information may still be used for functions such as frequency capping, fraud prevention, and aggregated reporting.

Game Progress and Local Data

Bug Defense may store game-related information locally on your device, including:

  • Unlocked levels
  • Unlocked towers
  • Game progress
  • Audio preferences
  • Advertising-related counters
  • Other gameplay settings

This information is primarily stored locally on the device using Unity's local storage mechanisms such as PlayerPrefs. We do not use this local gameplay data to identify you personally.

Removing the application or clearing its application data may delete locally stored game progress and settings.

Third-Party Services

  • Google AdMob — used to provide banner, interstitial, and rewarded advertisements.
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) — used to collect and manage advertising consent where required.

Third-party providers may collect and process information according to their own terms and privacy policies.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

Information may be processed or shared with third-party service providers where necessary to operate advertising, consent management, security, fraud prevention, analytics associated with advertising, or other application functionality.

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to limit the amount of information directly collected by Bug Defense. Most advertising-related data processing is handled by third-party services such as Google AdMob.

However, no internet-based service or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Children's Privacy

Bug Defense is not designed to intentionally collect personal information directly from children.

If the game is made available to children or younger audiences, advertising behavior and data processing may be subject to additional Google Play, Google AdMob, and applicable legal requirements.

User Choices

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have choices regarding how advertising-related information is processed. Where required, these choices are presented through Google's consent system.

You may also be able to reset or manage your advertising identifier through your Android device settings.

Data Retention

Bug Defense does not maintain a separate user account database for gameplay information.

Game progress stored locally remains on the device until it is changed, deleted, the application data is cleared, or the application is removed.

Third-party providers may retain advertising or technical information according to their own privacy policies and legal obligations.

International Data Processing

Third-party service providers such as Google may process information on servers located in different countries. The handling of such information is governed by the policies and safeguards of the relevant service provider.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when new features are added, advertising systems change, third-party services change, or legal or Google Play requirements change.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.
